    How to play

    A / D to move · ← / → to aim · W / S to set power · Q / E to curve · Space to roll. On touchscreens, drag to aim and tap to roll.

    Play five rounds per course. In group games, reaching the flag completes that course for you. When everyone finishes, the next course begins early. Your best throw on each of the three courses counts toward your total.
